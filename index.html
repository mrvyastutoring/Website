Q7. Solve 2x² + 5x − 3 = 0. Give your answers to 2 decimal places. (4 marks)

x = −5 ± √13
x = (−5 ± √(25+24)) / 4
x = (−5 ± 7) / 4
x = 0.5  or  x = −3

Hello, I'm Mr Vyas.

I'm a current secondary school maths teacher with three years' classroom experience, teaching KS3 and GCSE students across a wide range of abilities and backgrounds. I have extensive experience supporting students with SEND, and I adapt every lesson to the way the individual student learns.

My lessons use clear, structured presentations and carefully chosen worksheets and exam-style questions. We start with a short recap of previous learning, work through examples together, then build up to independent practice — so students feel comfortable asking questions and making mistakes along the way.

My focus isn't just on grades. I want students to genuinely understand the maths, explain their own reasoning, and build the confidence to tackle problems on their own.
